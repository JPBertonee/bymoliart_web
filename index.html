About

My name is Sofi. I was born and raised in Argentina, but for many years I have been traveling and living in different countries. I currently live in Australia, where I’ve been for almost two years—a place that deeply inspires me with its lush nature, the sea, and incredible wildlife.

I trained as an architect and also studied Visual Arts, a path I didn’t complete but that confirmed something I’ve always known: art has to be part of my life.

I have been drawing and creating for as long as I can remember. Over the past two years, I have chosen to fully follow my passion: designing digital illustrations and prints. I specialize in colorful, energetic, and joyful prints, created for fashion and textile brands.

I find inspiration in my everyday surroundings: flowers, objects, landscapes, and my travels. All of this transforms into unique and original pieces, through which I aim to bring identity and personality to each design.

I currently create digital prints and illustrations for brands seeking original, colorful designs with their own unique identity, turning every idea into a one-of-a-kind piece.
