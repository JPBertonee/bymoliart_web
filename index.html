About

I’m a surface pattern designer and illustrator creating expressive, joyful, contemporary patterns for fashion and textile brands. I design with real products in mind — how the print moves on the body, how the colors translate on fabric, and how a collection feels when it all comes together.

My work is inspired by organic shapes, playful details and bold-yet-soft color stories. I love building collections that feel modern, feminine and full of character — ready for licensing or custom collaborations.
